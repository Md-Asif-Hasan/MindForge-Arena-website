Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2024

1. Information We Collect

MindForge Arena collects the following types of information:

  • Account information (email, display name, profile photo)
  • Performance data (IQ scores, test results, completion times)
  • Usage statistics (time spent, features used)
  • Device information (for app optimization)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Track your progress and provide personalized recommendations
  • Enable competitive features like leaderboards and battles
  • Send important updates and notifications
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Service providers who assist in app operations
  • Analytics partners (in anonymized form)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

MindForge Arena uses Google AdMob to display third-party advertisements within the app. Please note the following:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google's advertising platform to serve ads. Google may use cookies and other technologies to collect and use information about your app usage to serve personalized ads based on your interests.
  • Advertising ID: The app may collect and use your device's Advertising ID (GAID) for advertising purposes. This identifier is used to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance.
  • Third-Party Ad Networks: Google AdMob may work with third-party advertising companies to serve ads. These companies may use their own cookies and tracking technologies to collect information about your interactions with ads.
  • Data Collection: Information collected for advertising purposes may include device identifiers, usage patterns, and location data (if permitted). This data is used to improve ad relevance and measure campaign effectiveness.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized ads by adjusting your device's advertising settings or by visiting Google's Ads Settings page. Note that opting out may result in seeing less relevant ads.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out of certain data collection
  • Update your profile information

7. Children's Privacy

MindForge Arena is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via email or in-app notification.
